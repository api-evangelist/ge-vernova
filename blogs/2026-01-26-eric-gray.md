Eric Gray Kim Leonard Mon, 01/26/2026 - 13:08 Eric is the Chief Executive Officer of GE Vernova's Power segment, which provides a wide-range of industry-leading power generation options including: gas turbines, steam turbines, advanced, small modular reactors and hydroelectric solutions to a host of traditional and non-traditional customers globally. Eric has more than 25 years of GE Vernova experience. Since joining GE Vernova in 2001 as a plant chemist, he has held a variety of commercial and operations leadership roles across products, services and projects. In recent years, he served as the President & CEO of our Gas Power business, North America Region General Manager for Power Services, General Manager for Multiyear Agreement sales, and General Manager of Gas Plant Solutions for GE Vernova’s Power Services business. In 2017, he was named Commercial Leader for Power Services. In 2022, Eric was named the President & CEO of the Gas Power business after serving for four years as CEO of GE Vernova's Gas Power business in the Americas. He was responsible for selling new units and services, servicing equipment and executing projects. He also served as the CEO of the global $4B Heavy Duty Gas Turbine Equipment business, overseeing commercial operations through project execution to deliver gas power generation across the globe. Eric is involved in the Atlanta community, where he is based, serving on the Metro Atlanta Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors. He holds a bachelor’s degree in chemistry from Indiana University and an MBA from Georgia State University.
